## Off-Campus Housing resources
There is a residential area in Technopolis, the area around DGIST. There are various types of housing such as apartments, villas, multi-family housings, etc.
If you would like to seek your house outside of campus, you should use a real estate agent or smartphone applications for housing seeking such as Jigbang (직방) and Dabang (다방).
