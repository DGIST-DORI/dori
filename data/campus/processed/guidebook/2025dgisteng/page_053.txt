## Using a Laundry App at Student Dormitory
For laundry at student dormitory, you should download the app called 'MetaClub', which allows you to pay for the laundry and check the available time and machine. International students or visiting students without a Residence Card can charge the money only with cash at a convenience store. Please see a guide for student without RC.
Dormitory: dormitory@dgist.ac.kr.
