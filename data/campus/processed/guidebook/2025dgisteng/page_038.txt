## DISA International Student Association
DISA, the DGIST International Student Association, established in 2024, is the first official organization representing the international community at DGIST. It supports international students, faculty, researchers, staff, and their families, fostering a welcoming and inclusive environment.
## DISA participation and contact
For those interested in volunteering or participating in DISA activities, you are welcome to visit the Global Lounge (E7-261) or contact us via email.
