# 02 Climate and Clothing
Korea has very distinctive four seasons; spring, summer, autumn, and winter. The summers are very long, hot, rainy, and wet. The winters are freezing, dry, and mostly clear with some snow. When bringing clothes to Korea, please pack clothes that can prepare for extreme weather. From light clothing such as t-shirts to winter coats and jackets.

Korean culture is characterized by harmony between modernity and tradition. Korean people rapidly embrace new technology and culture, but at the same time, they set store by courtesy to others and respect for the elders following the long traditions. In the cities, rows of skyscrapers and picturesque traditional buildings are harmonized well. The citizens have decent law-abiding attitude based on strong sens of community from traditional culture and it is one of the key values have led the rapid progress of economy and social system of today’s Korea.
Also, the current Korean culture represented by K-pop, K-drama, K-food and etc. draws inspiration from Korean younger generation’s emotions and various cultures all over the world. The younger generations of Korea easily expose to different culture and actively create something new with the high accessibility to internet and cutting-edge technology.

# 03 Currency
Korea’s official currency is won (KRW). The Korean currency consists of a one thousand, five thousand, ten thousand, fifty thousand won note and ten, fifty, hundred, five hundred won coins. There are also one won, five won coins but these are little used currently.
Source: https://blog.khetimes.com
Source : globalexchangebrasil.com.br

## Money Exchange
Certain currencies, such as US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), Japan Yen (JPY), China Yuan (CNY), etc. can be exchanged to Korean won by visiting a bank or an authorized exchange service center. Wherever you visit, please note that customer rates and commissions apply.
If you wish to bring money to Korea, it is recommended to bring either the US dollar or Euro rather than your own currency. It is because most of the time, the two currencies have lower cost than other.

## Credit / Debit Card
Most of the business in Korea widely use and accept payment by credit cards. Visa, Master, American Express and other credit cards can be used.

# 04 Power plugs
In Korea, the power plugs and sockets are types C and F. The standard voltage is 220V. The outlet with two round holes is also used in countries like France, Germany, Austria, etc.
Source : https://world-power-plugs.com

## Usim Card

Purchase and activation are possible at Incheon International Airport and Daegu International Airport. Additionally, activation through visiting an agent after online reservation is also possible. # 06 T-money

## Purchase of a T-money card at the airport 16
From April 1, 2025, all city buses in Daegu will operate on a 'cash-free' basis. Passengers will be able to pay the fare only with a transportation card. We highly recommend purchasing a 'T-money Card' at a convenience store (e.g., GS25, CU) in the airport and loading it with cash. The T-money Card costs between KRW 2,000 and KRW 5,000, and the minimum recharge amount is KRW 1,000. It can be used for buses, the metro, some taxis, and even as a debit card at convenience stores.

# 07 KakaoTalk

## Sign Up for KakaoTalk
Kakao Talk is the most popular messenger app in Korea. To have a chat with your lab colleagues to get useful information in Korea, you should download KakaoTalk and let your mentor know your KakaoTalk ID. Please note that you should download and sign up for KakaoTalk in your home countrysince it would not be convenient to do it in Korea without a local phone number.

## Disclaimer on the use of Kakao talk
If you've lost your phone or are unable to access KakaoTalk, you can log in by entering the verification code sent to your phone in your home country. However, please note that if you're in a different country (already in Korea), you might not receive the code. Therefore, it's recommended to set up a backup account on your laptop or iPad. Additionally, KakaoTalk has a policy that allows only one account per phone number and device, so you cannot log in on two phones and must use different devices.
In case you didn’t set up a backup account on other devices, the only option you would have is to login by using your email and register your new phone number that you will get in Korea or send verification code to your sim card in your home country then ask someone to send you the verification code. You can also login by using you email and register anyone’s phone number in your country and get the verification code from them since one number for one account policy would still work.

# 15 How to purchase AREX (Airport Railroad Express) tickets
To purchase AREX (Airport Railroad Express) tickets, you can buy them online, at ticket vending machines, or at the information counter. For online purchases, visit the official AREX website or app, select your route and time, and make a payment. You can also buy tickets at the AREX Information Center. 36
Here's a more detailed breakdown:
1. Online Purchase
• Website/App: Go to the official AREX website or app www.airportrailroad.com.
• Select Route: Input your departure (Incheon Airport or Seoul Station) and arrival stations, date, and time.
• Choose Train: Select your desired train schedule.
• Payment: Complete the payment process.
• Ticket Retrieval: You can either print your receipt or view your ticket on the app.
2. Ticket Vending Machines
• Location: Find the Express Train Ticket Vending Machines near the AREX platforms at Seoul Station, Incheon Airport Terminal 1, and Incheon Airport Terminal 2.
• Purchase: Follow the on-screen instructions to select your destination, boarding time, and number of passengers.
• Payment: Pay for your ticket using cash or card.
3. Information Counter
• Location: Visit the AREX Information Center at Seoul Station, Incheon Airport Terminal 1, or Incheon Airport Terminal 2.
• Purchase: Inform the staff of your desired route and time, and they will assist you with your purchase.
• Payment: Both cash and card payments are accepted.

• Discounts: Check for potential discounts when purchasing online or through travel agencies.
• Discover Seoul Pass: If you have a Discover Seoul Pass, you can use it to obtain your AREX ticket.

#### 20 Comparison: KTX vs. SRT
This document compares two major high-speed train services in South Korea: KTX (Korea Train Express) and SRT (Super Rapid Train).

<table><tr> <td> Feature</td> <td> KTX (Korea Train Express)</td> <td> SRT (Super Rapid Train)</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Operator</td> <td> Korail (Government-owned)</td> <td> SR Corporation (Private, Korail subsidiary)</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Start of Service</td> <td> 2004</td> <td> 2016</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Main Stations</td> <td> Seoul Station / Yongsan Station</td> <td> Suseo Station (Southeast Seoul, Gangnam area)</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Major Routes</td> <td> Seoul ↔ Busan / Mokpo / Gwangju / etc.</td> <td> Suseo ↔ Busan / Gwangju / Daegu / etc.</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Ticket Price</td> <td> Slightly more expensive</td> <td> Generally 5~10% cheaper than KTX</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Travel Time</td> <td> Comparable (Some routes: KTX slightly faster)</td> <td> Comparable (Some routes: SRT slightly faster)</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Comfort/Interior</td> <td> Similar (both offer economy & first class)</td> <td> Similar</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Wi-Fi</td> <td> Available (but varies by train model)</td> <td> Available</td> </tr>
<tr> <td> Target Users</td> <td> General national access via central Seoul</td> <td> Gangnam-based users needing direct access to south</td> </tr></table>

##### Summary:
• Use KTX if you're closer to Seoul Station or Yongsan Station.
• Use SRT if you're near Gangnam (Suseo Station) or want cheaper fares.
• Both are fast, efficient, and reliable for long-distance travel across South Korea. LIVING GUIDE FOR INTERNATIONAL MEMBERS 47 Things You Need to Know before Arrival

• Key Differences:
- KTX (Korea Train eXpress)
» A high-speed express train operating at up to 300 km/h. It departs from Seoul Station, Yongsan Station, and Cheongnyangni Station. Connects major cities such as Busan, Mokpo, etc.
- SRT (Super Rapid Train)
» Another high-speed train, also running at up to 300 km/h. It departs from Suseo Station in southern Seoul. Often offers slightly lower fares compared to KTX.
- Saemaeul Train
» A premium-class conventional train that departs from Seoul Station, Yongsan Station and Cheongnyangni Station.
- Mugunghwa Train
» A standard conventional train that also departs from Seoul’s major stations. It stops at smaller regional stations that are often skipped by KTX and other premium trains. A good option for local travel. LIVING GUIDE FOR INTERNATIONAL MEMBERS 57 Things You Need to Know before Arrival

- You can easily find the schedule and make reservation via the mobile application, Korail.
• Official website:
- Korail https://www.korail.com
- SRT https://etk.srail.kr
- Both websites support English, Chinese and Japanese. You can search routes, choose seats, and pay by credit card. An e-ticket with a QR code will be issued immediately after payment.
• How to Use the KTX (Korail) & SRT Mobile Apps

## 1. Download the App
- Korail Talk (코레일톡): Search “Korail Talk” on the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).
- SRT 앱: Search “SRT” or “SRT 수서고속철도” on the App Store or Google Play.
- Both apps support English, although some parts may appear in Korean.

## 2. Search for a Train
- Open the app and choose the “Schedule” or “Ticket Reservation” menu.
- Enter your departure and arrival stations (e.g., Seoul → Busan), travel date, and departure time.
- Tap Search to see a list of available trains and seat types (Standard / First Class).

## 3. Choose a Train & Seat
- Select a train from the list based on time and price.
- Choose your seat direction (forward/backward) and seat class, if available.

## 4. Make a Payment
- Proceed to the payment screen.
- Enter your passport number if required.
- Pay with a credit or debit card (Visa, MasterCard, etc.).

## 5. Receive Your Ticket
- After payment, you will receive an e-ticket with a QR code.
- You can access your ticket anytime under the “My Ticket” or “Reservation” tab.

## 6. Boarding the Train
- Arrive at the station at least 10 minutes early.
- Use your QR code to enter through the ticket gate (no printing necessary).
- Find your car number and seat number as indicated in the ticket. LIVING GUIDE FOR INTERNATIONAL MEMBERS 59 Things You Need to Know before Arrival

# Tips
- You can also buy tickets at self-service ticket vending machines located inside major train stations (note: not available at subway stations). These machines typically offer English language support and accept cards.
* However, on weekends and public holidays, tickets may sell out quickly due to high demand, making it difficult to get a desired departure time and possibly requiring a wait for the next available train.
- Tickets can usually be purchased from one month in advance up to 20 minutes before departure.
- Some trains have seats positioned in two directions, forward and backward, which you can choose depending on your preference and situation. If you are sensitive to motion sickness, please keep this in mind when booking your seat.
- KTX and SRT amenities include restrooms, nursing rooms, diaper changing tables and vending machines. Most trains also provide free Wi-Fi service. 60

# How To Order On Baedal Minjok (배달의민족)05 Using A Foreign Credit Card
This guide will walk you through the process step-by-step. *IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER: Do NOT log in to the Baedal Minjok app. If you do, you will not be able to use a foreign credit card and phone number. Always use the app without logging in to access the foreign card payment option.

## Step 1: Open the Baedal Minjok App
Start by opening the Baedal Minjok app on your smartphone. You should see the main menu without logging in. 96

## Step 2: Choose Your Delivery Address
Select your delivery address by tapping on the address field at the top of the screen. You may need to allow the app to access your location or enter the address manually.
The Crucial Importance of Accurate Address Information
When using Baedal Minjok as a foreigner, one aspect cannot be overstated: the absolute necessity of entering your delivery address accurately and completely.
Why Is This So Important?
• Language Barrier: Most delivery drivers in Korea speak only Korean. If they can’t find your location, they will likely call you, and communication can be challenging if you don’t speak the language.
• Detailed Addressing System: Korean addresses are often very specific, including street names, numbers, building names and unit numbers.
• Time Sensitivity: Food delivery is time-sensitive, and incorrect addresses can lead to cold food or even cancelled orders.
How to Ensure Address Accuracy:
• Include Everything: Make sure to input your complete address, including:
-Building name (if applicable)
-House/apartment number
-Street name and number
-Neighborhood or district
• You can use this website to convert an English street address in Korea to its official Korean version (or vice versa). However, you will still need to add the building name and house/unit number.
Use Korean if Possible: If you can, have your address written in Korean.
• Add Notes: Use the ‘additional instructions’ or ‘notes’ section to provide extra details that might help the driver locate you easily.
• Double-Check: Before finalizing your order, review your address information carefully.

## Step 3: Select Your Food and Add to Cart
Browse through the restaurants and select the items you want to order. Add them to your cart when you’re ready to check out.

## Step 4: Proceed to Payment
Once you’re satisfied with your order, proceed to the order section. You’ll see various payment options.
• You might see a popup asking you to log in or create an account. Remember our important disclaimer:
*Do NOT log in or create an account. Instead, look for an option to continue as a guest or simply close the popup. This is crucial to maintain your ability to use a foreign credit card. LIVING GUIDE FOR INTERNATIONAL MEMBERS 97 Amenities near Campus

## Step 5: Enter Your Phone Number
You’ll be prompted to enter a phone number.
• The great news is that you can enter your foreign phone number here. Make sure to include the country code (e.g., +1 for the United States) before your actual phone number. 98

## Step 6: Select Foreign Credit Card Option
Scroll down the payment options and look for “해외 신용카드” (Foreign Credit Card). This is the option you need to select to use your foreign-issued credit card.

## Step 7: Enter Your Credit Card Details
Now you can enter your foreign credit card information:
- Card number
- Expiration date
- C-VV (security code)
• Double-check all the information to ensure it’s correct.

## Step 8: Complete Your Order
Review your order one last time, then confirm the payment. You may be redirected to your card issuer’s verification page, depending on your bank’s security measures.
• Remember, the success of your food delivery experience largely depends on the accuracy of your address. Taking a few extra moments to ensure all details are correct can save you from potential frustration and ensure you get to enjoy your delicious Korean meal without any hiccups!
• By following this guide and paying special attention to your address details, you’re all set to enjoy the convenience and flavors of Korean food delivery through Baedal Minjok. Happy eating!

Immigration Immigration

# 01 VISA
If you are planning to study or work in Korea, you must get appropriate type of visa from the Korean embassy in each country. Required documents and application process may different depending on purpose and period of your stay and nationality. Therefore, it is recommended that you check the eligible type of visa and what is required for applying in beforehand

## More Information & Inquiries 118
• Ministry of Foreign Affairs : mofa.go.kr
• Korea Visa Portal : www.visa.go.kr/main
• Hi Korea : www.hikorea.go.kr
• 1345 Call Center : 1345 (no area code), 18 language provided *available in Korea

# 02 Residence Card
Every foreigner staying in Korea for more than 90 days MUST create an Residence Card. Individual who need to apply for Residence Card have to visit the immigration office.
Application Period : within 90 days from your arrival date

## Required Document
• Application form
• Passport
• 1 colored photo taken within 6 months (size: 3.5 cm x 4.5 cm, taken against a white background)
• Certificate of Enrollment (students) / Certificate of Employment (faculty & researcher)
• Proof of Residency
• Commission fee (30,000 KRW in cash)

## Note
• When you fill out the application form, please write your address (including the building and room number of your dormitory) and full name accurately. If you don’t write exact information on the application, you may be not able to get the posts or notification.
• At the beginning of the semester, the International Affairs Team (IAT) will organize a group application for Residence Cards for new students.
The IAT will arrange the time, transportation, and other necessary details, so anyone can participate in this support.

# 03 Dependent Family (F-3 Visa)
If you want to invite your family, your family members have to get F-3 visa. They can apply for the visa at Korean embassy in your country.

## Required Document
• Application form • 2 Colored photo taken within 6 month
• Commission fee(130,000KRW)
• Certificate of marriage/birth (in Kor/Eng) • Copy of passport
• Copy of ID card & copies of 1~2 page of birth certificates (translated in English)
• Invitation Letter written by invitor, copies of the invitee's Residence Card (both front and back side), certificate of employment and salary statement (or balance of the bank account for the past three months)
• Visa applicants' certificate of employment (if they have jobs) or a certificate of enrollment (if they are students), a copy of the three-month bank balance and a confirmation of financial support from the applicant's parents or spouse (submit bank balance certificate & copy of the birth certificate issued in English)

# 04 VISA Extension
You can apply for the extension of stay four months before your visa’s expiration date. Please keep in mind that if you apply for the extension after your expiration date, you will be fined accordingly
How to Apply : Visit the local immigration office with the necessary documents or via Hi Korea web-site (www.hikorea.go.kr)

## Required Documents
• Application Form • Passport • Residence Card
• Renewed employment contract or a certificate of enrollment
• Proof of Residency
• Copy of DGIST business registration certificate
• Commission fee (60,000 KRW in cash)
* The immigration office may request additional document(s) at its discretion.)
* It will be issued by each department
