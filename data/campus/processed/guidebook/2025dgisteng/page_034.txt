## Korean Language Center
DGIST is expanding our global presence by creating a Bilingual Campus that brings together diverse perspectives. Our Korean Language Center was founded with the idea of encouraging meaningful connections between international students and Korean Students. DGIST believes that through language and culture, we can build a community where everyone grows together.
Homepage: https://www.dgist.ac.kr/klc/
## Korean Language Class overview
DGIST provides Korean language class not only for international students but other international members and their family. Two different levels of classes, one for beginner and the other for post-ginner, are offered per each semester. International students can take the courses after registration and, other members (faculties, researchers, staffs and long-term visitors) and their family can audit the courses.
