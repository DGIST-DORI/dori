## Summer/Winter Intensive TOPIK Class overview
At DGIST, we are pleased to offer the Intensive TOPIK Class during the vacation period, designed to support international members preparing for the Test of Proficiency in Korean (TOPIK). The course is divided into TOPIK I (beginner) and TOPIK II (intermediate/advanced) levels, focusing on problem-solving and exam preparation. This class provides targeted practice and strategies to help you excel in the TOPIK and improve your Korean language skills effectively.
## Language Exchange Program overview
We are excited to introduce a Language Exchange Program for students at DGIST, who are interested in learning and improving their Korean and English-speaking skills. To create a casual and creative learning experience, we intend to include various games, quizzes, etc., in both Korean and English to facilitate interaction among participants.
## Living in Korea Lecture Series overview
This session is designed to provide useful insights, cultural context, and practical tips to help international members better understand and adjust to life in Korea. This course covers topics related to Korean laws, culture, politics, economy, and society.
