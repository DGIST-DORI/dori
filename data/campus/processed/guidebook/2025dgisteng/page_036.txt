## Buddy Program
Here at DGIST, we welcome our international students through the Buddy program, where we match our students with other existing students. In this program, international students will be provided with a general introduction to life on campus and receive various support for settling in.
## Orientation for International Members
To help international members understand and adjust to life in DGIST and Korea, International Affairs Team (IAT) offers customized orientation to new international members.
Program: Overview on DGIST; facilities and services on Campus and in Daegu area; general guide to campus life; campus tour and campus map.
