## Power plugs
In Korea, the power plugs and sockets are types C and F. The standard voltage is 220V. The outlet with two round holes is also used in countries like France, Germany, Austria, etc.
## Prepaid SIM cards before arrival
Prepaid Sim Cards can be available for use with passports even before obtaining a Residence Card. Purchase and activation are possible at Incheon International Airport and Daegu International Airport. Additionally, activation through visiting an agent after online reservation is also possible. Please make sure to prepare the SimCard/e-Sim before coming to Korea or at the airport since it is quite difficult to get the prepaid SimCard/e-Sim without a Residence Card nearby DGIST.
